# Artwork and third-party credits

## Nintendo characters and artwork

The Legend of Zelda, Link, Zelda, Ganondorf, Navi, the Triforce, and associated artwork and marks belong to Nintendo. The project MIT license does not license these assets or grant rights to them. This is an unofficial fan theme.

- Link wallpaper: incorporates the original Ocarina of Time illustration, obtained from [Zelda Wiki](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/File:OoT_Link_Artwork.png). [Source PNG](https://cdn.wikimg.net/en/zeldawiki/images/2/29/OoT_Link_Artwork.png).
- Zelda wallpaper: AI-created cel-shaded illustration based on [Ocarina of Time Zelda artwork](https://zeldawiki.wiki/wiki/File:OoT_Adult_Zelda_Artwork.png).
- Ganondorf wallpaper: AI-created cel-shaded illustration based on [Ocarina of Time Ganondorf artwork](https://www.zeldadungeon.net/wiki/Ganondorf_(Ocarina_of_Time)).
- Sacred Realm wallpaper and screensaver: original fan compositions incorporating the Triforce and Navi.
